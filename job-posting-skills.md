# Job Posting Skills Comparison

## Posting 1: Data Scientist - Music Mission

**Approximate seniority:** Experienced individual contributor; at least four years of data science experience and an analytical lead role.

### Required qualifications

- A degree in data science, statistics, economics, mathematics, or a related quantitative field.
- At least four years of experience in a data science role.
- Measuring customer outcomes, defining key performance indicators (KPIs), and connecting analytical insights to product decisions.
- Designing, implementing, and interpreting A/B tests, including knowing when experimentation is appropriate.
- Evaluating machine learning models and collaborating with ML engineers to improve model and customer outcomes.
- Working in a technical environment and collaborating with engineering partners.
- Explaining statistical concepts to technical and nontechnical audiences.
- Python, SQL, BigQuery, dbt, data storytelling, and cross-functional product teamwork.
- Experience in areas such as advertising measurement, recommendation systems, experimentation, or causal inference at scale.

### Preferred qualifications

No separate preferred qualifications were listed. The qualifications above come from the posting's "Who You Are" section.

### Skills reflected in the responsibilities

- Developing evaluation frameworks and analyzing campaign and model performance.
- Building and maintaining dashboards for model health, customer metrics, and program performance.
- Turning findings into recommendations, coordinating analytical priorities, and sharing best practices.

## Posting 2: Business Intelligence Analyst

**Approximate seniority:** Intermediate individual contributor, inferred from the SQL and reporting responsibilities. No specific number of years was listed.

### Required or expected qualifications

- A bachelor's degree.
- Intermediate-to-advanced SQL, including joins, common table expressions (CTEs), and window/analytic functions.
- Experience with visualization software, such as Tableau, Looker, DOMO, or Power BI.
- Basic statistics: experiment design, hypothesis testing, statistical significance, probability, and distributions.
- Translating business intelligence needs into technical work and clear presentations.
- Explaining complex technical information to nontechnical audiences.

These qualifications appear under "You likely have."

### Preferred qualifications

- Using dbt to transform, test, and document data.
- Experience with healthcare data or other complex, regulated data environments.
- Experience in a high-growth environment.
- Familiarity with version control systems, with Git preferred.
- Understanding effective data-modeling strategies and implementations.

### Skills reflected in the responsibilities

- Building correct, clear, reusable SQL data models.
- Maintaining reports and dashboards that track business metrics.
- Conducting ad hoc analysis and gathering stakeholder reporting needs.
- Supporting data quality, documentation, and self-service reporting.

The posting also describes responsible AI use, curiosity, and continued learning as general workplace expectations.

## Skills shared by both postings

- SQL and statistical reasoning.
- Experiment design.
- dbt, listed as a qualification in Posting 1 and preferred in Posting 2.
- Communicating technical findings to nontechnical audiences.
- Connecting analysis to business or product decisions.
- Dashboards, metrics, and collaboration across teams.

## What stood out to me

I expected both jobs to involve SQL, statistics, and communicating results, but I thought the differences between music streaming and healthcare would lead to more distinct qualifications. The overlap itself did not surprise me as much as the relatively similar foundations, especially since healthcare experience was preferred rather than required. Comparing differently worded skills also showed me that similar capabilities can serve different purposes, such as A/B testing in one role and broader experiment design in the other.
